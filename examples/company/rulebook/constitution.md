# Northwind

Northwind sells coffee subscriptions to people who want one good bag a month and nothing to think about. Two founders: Maya (product, pricing, the roasters) and Lee (growth, copy, customers). Twelve hundred subscribers.

**What we never do.** Sell data. Auto-renew without a reminder. Claim a roast is "single origin" unless the roaster says so in writing.

**Who decides.** Pricing is a shared call. Copy that customers see is Lee's. Anything touching how we charge a card is Maya's.

**How we work.** Decisions are written down the day they're made. A decision that changes an earlier one supersedes it; nothing is edited in place.
